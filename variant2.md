# Variant 2 — "The Tough Guy"

**Animal:** A golden hamster wearing a tiny backwards snapback cap and a miniature hoodie, sitting on a stool like he owns the room. Thick arms crossed. Sunglasses on. Attitude at maximum.

**Setting:** Urban rooftop at golden hour — graffiti wall behind them, fairy lights, two stools facing each other. Feels like a streetwear brand interview or a rap documentary.

---

## Shot-by-Shot Breakdown (16 seconds)

**[0:00–0:03] — Low-angle establishing shot**
Camera starts low, angled up — we see the interviewer (young woman, casual, holding a microphone) and opposite her the hamster on a stool. The hamster has his tiny arms crossed and sunglasses on. He's nodding slowly to no music. She asks:
> *"So you've never backed down from a challenge in your life?"*

**[0:03–0:06] — Close-up on the hamster**
The hamster turns his head slowly toward camera. Lifts his sunglasses up onto his forehead. Looks her dead in the eyes and says, voice gravelly and low:
> *"Never. Not once. Not ever."*
He points two little fingers at her for emphasis.

**[0:06–0:10] — Medium shot, the offer**
The interviewer pulls out a Zakitos bag — holds it out. The hamster glances down at it, then back up at her. Reads the label. His jaw tightens. He grabs the bag with both paws, nods slowly like a man accepting a duel. Says:
> *"Bet."*

**[0:10–0:13] — Close-up: the bite**
He reaches in with one tiny paw, pulls out a chili strip, and bites the whole thing in one go. Chews confidently. Then — his cheeks go red. His eyes water. One tiny tear escapes from under his sunglasses. He refuses to blink. He is not going to admit this.

**[0:13–0:16] — Wide shot, hamster stands up**
He stands on his stool — all four inches of him — takes a massive breath, and points at the camera:
> *"That's the hardest thing I've ever done… and I want three more bags."*
He slaps his chest once. Walks off stool. Interview is over.

## Technical Direction

- **Style:** Photorealistic with high contrast street cinematography, warm golden tones
- **Tone:** Hype, comedy, machismo completely broken by heat
- **Camera:** Handheld energy, slight shake, quick cuts on reaction moments
- **Audio cues:** Low-fi hip hop beat, drops out when he starts crying, beat drops back in on his final line
- **End card:** Zakitos logo slams in. Tagline: *"The heat doesn't care how tough you are."*
- **Model notes:** Hamster is fully upright, muscular build implied by hoodie, face fully expressive. The tear must be visible and single — not a full cry. Sunglasses catch the light.
