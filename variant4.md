# Variant 4 — "The Breakout Star"

**Animal:** A chipmunk who is very clearly having the best day of his life. He is thrilled to be here. He has prepared. He brought notes. He is wearing a tiny clip-on bow tie that is slightly crooked.

**Setting:** A bright morning talk show set — pastel couch, coffee table with a mug that says "World's Hottest", fake plants, cheerful lighting. The host is a bubbly woman with a huge smile. The chipmunk is READY.

---

## Shot-by-Shot Breakdown (16 seconds)

**[0:00–0:03] — Bright talk show establishing shot**
Upbeat morning show music. Wide shot: the host is mid-laugh, the chipmunk is seated on a tiny cushion on the couch beside her, leaning forward enthusiastically, bow tie slightly crooked. Host composes herself and says:
> *"Okay, so you told our producers backstage that you consider yourself — and I quote — 'a heat connoisseur'?"*

**[0:03–0:06] — Close-up on the chipmunk**
The chipmunk nods vigorously, cheeks full (he's been stress-eating acorns backstage). He holds up one tiny finger and says very fast and very earnestly:
> *"Correct. I eat peppers recreationally. I once ate a jalapeño on a dare and I felt nothing. Absolutely nothing."*
He taps his chest proudly.

**[0:06–0:10] — The reveal**
The host reaches behind her and produces a Zakitos bag with a dramatic *"ta-da"* gesture. The chipmunk's eyes go from confident to — just for one frame — slightly less confident. He picks up the bag and reads the label very carefully. His ear twitches. He says, still confident:
> *"Fine. Fine. Yes. Let's do this."*
He pulls out a strip.

**[0:10–0:13] — The bite**
He takes a big proud bite. Chews twice. And then his entire body goes rigid. His eyes cross slightly. His bow tie pops off from the tension in his neck. He doesn't move. He doesn't blink. The host leans in, concerned. He holds up one tiny finger — *wait* — he's processing.

**[0:13–0:16] — The verdict, delivered with full conviction**
He slowly uncrosses his eyes. He turns to the camera. In a hoarse but utterly committed voice, he announces:
> *"I am a changed chipmunk. Everything before this was a warm-up."*
He takes the entire bag. The host claps. He holds the bag up like a trophy.

---

## Technical Direction

- **Style:** Bright, high-key morning TV aesthetic — warm whites, soft fill lights, very clean
- **Tone:** Pure joy, chaos energy, earnest commitment to the bit
- **Camera:** Standard multi-camera talk show cuts — wide, medium, close-up on reactions. Quick zoom on bow tie popping off.
- **Audio cues:** Upbeat morning show theme, crowd "ooh" on the reveal, complete silence on the rigid freeze moment, then applause on his final line
- **End card:** Zakitos logo on pastel background. Tagline: *"Changed lives. One strip at a time."*
- **Model notes:** The chipmunk must be expressive and slightly manic with excitement. The bow tie popping off is a critical physical comedy beat — needs to be clearly visible. Cheeks appropriately chubby.
