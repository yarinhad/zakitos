# Variant 3 — "The Nature Documentary"

**Animal:** A field mouse in his natural habitat — but he is fully aware of the camera and deeply uncomfortable with it. No clothes. Just a regular wild mouse, but he speaks and has opinions.

**Setting:** A miniature interview setup built inside a hedge or garden — tiny folding chair, a blade of grass as a microphone, an acorn as a desk ornament. Feels like David Attenborough accidentally got too close.

---

## Shot-by-Shot Breakdown (16 seconds)

**[0:00–0:03] — Nature documentary wide shot**
Sweeping cinematic shot of a garden hedge. A deep British narrator voice says:
> *"In the wild, the field mouse faces many predators. Today — he faces something far more dangerous."*
Camera slowly zooms in on a tiny interview setup in the roots of the hedge. A human interviewer is seated cross-legged on the ground, lowered to eye level with the mouse.

**[0:03–0:06] — Cut to the mouse**
The mouse is in his little chair, clearly nervous. He keeps glancing off-camera. He looks back at the interviewer. She asks gently:
> *"How would you describe your relationship with spicy food?"*
The mouse straightens up, clears his throat, and says in a very proper, slightly squeaky voice:
> *"Nonexistent. I eat seeds. I am a seed man."*

**[0:06–0:10] — The offer, extreme tension**
The interviewer slowly reaches over and places one Zakitos chili strip on the ground directly in front of the mouse. He stares at it. Camera does a slow dramatic push-in on the chili strip. Then on the mouse's face. Then back to the chili strip. The narrator whispers:
> *"The mouse considers his options."*

**[0:10–0:13] — The bite, slow motion**
The mouse leans forward, sniffs it three times, then picks it up with both paws and takes a slow, committed bite. Slow-motion capture. His eyes go absolutely huge. His whiskers splay outward like a star.

**[0:13–0:16] — Post-bite, looking into distance**
Camera cuts to the mouse staring off into the middle distance, chewing slowly. He looks like a man who has seen things. He finally speaks, very quietly:
> *"I am no longer a seed man."*
He takes another bite. The narrator says: *"Nature is full of surprises."*

---

## Technical Direction

- **Style:** BBC nature documentary cinematography — ultra-realistic, shallow depth of field, natural light
- **Tone:** Absurdist, deadpan, British dry humor
- **Camera:** Mimics wildlife documentary technique — slow zooms, patient holds, reverent push-ins
- **Audio cues:** Full orchestral nature doc score, hushed narrator, complete silence during mouse's final line
- **End card:** Zakitos logo over a nature scene. Tagline: *"Not just for humans."*
- **Model notes:** The mouse is NOT anthropomorphized in appearance — he looks like a real wild mouse. Only his speech and awareness of the camera is human. This contrast is the entire joke.
