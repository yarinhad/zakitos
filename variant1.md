# Variant 1 — "The Food Critic"

**Animal:** A well-dressed white mouse wearing tiny round glasses and a miniature turtleneck sweater, seated in a velvet armchair across from the interviewer.

**Setting:** A sleek, dimly lit interview set — black marble table between them, warm spotlight, bokeh background of city lights. Feels like a Michelin-star restaurant review show.

---

## Shot-by-Shot Breakdown (16 seconds)

**[0:00–0:03] — Wide establishing shot**
Camera pulls back to reveal both subjects: a polished male interviewer in a dark blazer leaning forward with a microphone, and across from him — a tiny white mouse, sitting upright in a miniature velvet chair, paws crossed, wearing tiny glasses. The mouse looks mildly bored. Interviewer says:
> *"You've reviewed 400 snacks this year. What does it take to impress you?"*

**[0:03–0:06] — Close-up on the mouse**
Camera cuts to tight close-up on the mouse's face. He adjusts his glasses with one tiny paw, sighs through his nose, and says in a refined, slightly exhausted voice:
> *"Nothing. Nothing impresses me anymore."*
His tiny whiskers twitch once.

**[0:06–0:10] — Medium shot, interviewer leans in**
Interviewer reaches under the desk and places a single Zakitos 1-Pack bag — perfectly sized relative to the mouse — on the small table in front of him. Camera slowly pushes in on the bag. Interviewer says:
> *"What about… Zakitos?"*
The mouse's eyes dart to the bag. His composure cracks — barely, but it's there.

**[0:10–0:13] — Extreme close-up: the mouse eats**
The mouse opens the tiny bag with both paws, removes one dried chili strip, sniffs it carefully, and takes a single dignified bite. Extreme close-up on his tiny chewing mouth. His eyes go wide. His glasses slide down his nose.

**[0:13–0:16] — Cut back to wide shot, mouse stands up**
The mouse stands up in his little chair, removes his glasses, places them on the table, and looks directly into the camera. He says, completely deadpan:
> *"Cancel my other appointments."*
He picks up the whole bag and exits frame. Interviewer stares, stunned.

## Technical Direction

- **Style:** Photorealistic, cinematic, soft studio lighting
- **Tone:** Dry comedy, refined, understated
- **Camera:** Slow smooth cuts, slight push-ins on key moments
- **Audio cues:** Soft jazz underscore, stops dead when mouse speaks
- **End card:** Zakitos logo fades in over black. Tagline: *"Even the harshest critics can't resist."*
- **Model notes:** Mouse must be fully anthropomorphized — upright posture, expressive face, articulated paws. Clothes must fit perfectly. No cartoon style.
